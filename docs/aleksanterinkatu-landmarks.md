# Aleksanterinkatu landmarks

The starting checkpoint is `62ff149` on `bekharsky/aleksanterinkatu-runner`. The worktree was clean before this refinement.

The route runs west to east. North is on the player's left. Landmark sides and relative order follow the street; frontage lengths and gaps are compressed to keep the 640-metre arcade course and its four crossing events. This is a stylised reconstruction, not a surveyed model.

The City Museum's visitor address is Aleksanterinkatu 16; the individual Sederholm House is number 18. Reference photographs are used for research only and are not distributed as game assets. All new geometry is generated locally. Facade lettering and shop signs are omitted.

`maps/aleksi-landmarks.js` owns the individual models and their reserved frontages. The generic facade builder subtracts these spans before filling the remaining blocks, so buildings do not overlap or close the final waterfront opening. Side elevations have windows too. The end-of-street vista is a deliberately compressed arcade waterfront: a broad quay, a left turn of both tram tracks along the shoreline, lamps, terminal and cruise ferry. The cathedral remains on the separate Senate Square map. The terminal sits inland of the bend; the quay is wide enough for both tracks and the whole tram body. The two cars follow the shared route separately, and their collision and liquid blockers follow their actual positions. This runner tram is a different, longer green-and-cream model from the green-and-yellow tram shared by the three arena maps.

Geometry remains merged by building, within the 563,701-vertex budget of checkpoint `62ff149`.

The map tests check landmark sides, the opposite Pohjola/Lundqvist pair, lane clearance, the low Sederholm roofline, non-overlapping frontages, open crossing corridors, the geometry budget and exactly-once resource disposal. Existing runner tests continue to cover safe routes at 15/30/60/120 FPS, tram collision, lateral scooter events and changes between braking and boosting. Cross-street road spans replace the longitudinal surface instead of overlapping it; warning arrows pulse smoothly and are the sole crossing notifications; runner toasts are hidden. Both runners accept vertical joystick and W/S or arrow-key speed input (45–150% of cruise), with a smoothed camera lens and hero lean following achieved speed, and dodging riders awards 150 points compared with 50 for spraying them. Visual checks use the actual game camera, lights, hero scale and renderer.

## Street and market refinement

The generic fronts now distinguish shop portals from upper-storey windows, with stone bases, cornices, projecting bays, balconies, pitched roofs and dormers. Stockmann's narrow window bays are divided by brick spandrels rather than looking like one continuous glass wall. Signs remain omitted.

The open approach is furnished as an arcade interpretation of a Helsinki market quarter: a red-brick pavilion, produce and flower stalls, cafe tables, visitors, planter trees and parked bicycles. The last row of stalls sits within the actual finishing camera view. A small fountain uses the pink stone, bronze maiden and four sea-lion motifs of [Havis Amanda (HAM)](https://www.hamhelsinki.fi/en/sculptures/havis-amanda-2/). Additional context comes from [the City of Helsinki's Market Square project](https://www.hel.fi/en/urban-environment-and-traffic/urban-planning-and-construction/plans-and-building-projects/market-square) and [the Old Market Hall's own history](https://vanhakauppahalli.fi/en/story/). This is a compressed setting rather than the real placement of the hall or fountain on Aleksanterinkatu. Two small launches add depth to the water around the ferry.

All exposed street, crossing, sidewalk, market and quay surfaces use generated granite setts. One colour texture and one height texture are shared by three paving batches. World-coordinate UVs keep stone scale and joints consistent between patches; mipmaps and anisotropic filtering reduce shimmer while moving. Road spans still stop at cross streets, so surfaces do not become coplanar. No photographic textures are bundled. Market props share one vertex-coloured material.

The market tests verify paving coverage and shared texture disposal, the free running corridor, and clearance between every market prop and both articulated tram paths. Visual checks cover the street, crossing, market approach and finish.

## Crossing scooter challenge

The two lateral scooters use different lines through each square (centre −6 m and +7 m) at 18 and 20 m/s. Their timing first threatens the source-side lane and then the opposite lane, with offsets that leave a dodge route even at full braking or boost. Remaining in either lane costs a hit; taking both dangerous lines costs two. Contacts each cost one health point, and crossing contacts grant only 0.45 s of protection. A red pooled impact burst makes the damage visible without a toast. Scooters have 1.8 health: a single stray splash cannot remove them, while a charged direct hit still can. Normal cyclists keep their previous health.
