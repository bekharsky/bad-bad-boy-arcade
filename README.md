# Golden Blast Helsinki

Bad Bad Boy is loose in Helsinki. Take the city's cheekiest sculpture through lively squares, waterfront streets and two fast runs, armed with a very personal spray attack.

**[Play Golden Blast](https://bekharsky.github.io/bad-bad-boy-arcade/)**

## The journey

Fight your way through **West Harbor**, **Kamppi** and **Senate Square**. Barnacle geese and Spanish slugs arrive in growing waves, while seagulls circle overhead and dive when you least want them to. Find medkits tucked around the neighbourhood and Battery cans that turn your spray into a bright triple stream.

Between the squares, race down **Baana** and **Aleksanterinkatu**. Change lanes to slip past cyclists, scooters, roadworks and trams; spray a path through the moving traffic. The route changes with every run, and your score carries into the next district.

The city is an affectionate arcade version of Helsinki: a ferry and cranes at the harbour, market stalls and landmark façades along Aleksanterinkatu, the Chapel of Silence in Kamppi, and the cathedral above Senate Square. Look for the little details in the houses and the playful trees along the way.

## Controls

| | Desktop | Touchscreen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move and dodge | Arrow keys or WASD | Left joystick |
| Spray | Space or mouse button; hold for more range | Right spray button; hold for more range |
| Aim at seagulls | Hold E while spraying | Drag upward from the spray button |
| Pause | Esc | Controls button |

In the running districts, left and right change lanes; up and down accelerate or brake. The Controls menu also offers movement, aiming and sound options.
