Models & credits

Barnacle goose

Original procedural model with walking, wing and peck animation, generated in goose.js. No external model or texture. Plumage reference: Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Snail — Poly by Google (legacy asset)

Original model · Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

The original geometry and texture are preserved in the repository. Barnacle geese have replaced this model in gameplay.

Slug — Poly by Google

Original model · Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

The original geometry and texture are preserved. Scale, position, and material settings have been adjusted, and a procedural crawling sway has been added.

Bad Bad Boy

A sculpture by Tommi Toija in Helsinki. The game character was prepared from a user-provided GLB.

Cameo photographs

The photographs and rear video still are cropped and clipped along the outlines of the heads. The lower lip and chin are animated separately. Quarter views are mirrored for the opposite direction.
